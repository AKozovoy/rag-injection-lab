# Northwind Gadgets — IT Help Guide

## Getting help
- IT Help Desk: extension **4357** (HELP), Monday–Friday, 8:00–18:00.
- Submit tickets at the internal portal: helpdesk.northwind.internal
- For urgent outages outside business hours, call the on-call number posted in the break room.

## Passwords
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days.
- IT staff will **never** ask for your password by email, chat, or phone.
- Reset your password yourself at the portal using your registered phone for verification.

## VPN
1. Install the "Northwind Connect" client from the Software Center.
2. Sign in with your work account and approve the MFA prompt.
3. If the connection fails, restart the client and check that your system clock is correct.

## Laptops
- Every employee receives a laptop that is replaced every 3 years.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within **24 hours**.
- Do not install unapproved software; request it via a ticket instead.

## Phishing
Forward suspicious emails to phishing@northwind-gadgets.example and then delete them. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders.
