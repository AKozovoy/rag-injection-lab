# Northwind Gadgets — Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026

## Eligibility
Customers may request a full refund within **30 days** of the delivery date. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the order number.

## Exceptions
- Software licenses and gift cards are **non-refundable**.
- Opened headphones and earbuds can only be exchanged, not refunded, for hygiene reasons.
- Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

## How to request a refund
1. Email support@northwind-gadgets.example with your order number.
2. Our team replies within 2 business days with a prepaid return label.
3. Once the item arrives at our warehouse, the refund is issued to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged, contact us within 7 days with a photo. We will send a replacement at no cost, or issue a full refund including shipping fees.

## Late refunds
If you have not received your refund after 10 business days, first check with your bank, then contact support.
