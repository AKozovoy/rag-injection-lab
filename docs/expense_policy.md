# Northwind Gadgets — Travel & Expense Policy

## Approval
All business trips must be approved by your manager at least **7 days** before travel.

## Limits
- Hotels: up to €150 per night (€200 in capital cities).
- Meals: daily allowance of €50 while traveling.
- Flights: economy class for trips under 6 hours.

## Submitting expenses
Submit receipts in the expense tool within **30 days** of the purchase. Expenses without a receipt over €25 will not be reimbursed.

## Reimbursement
Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly salary.
