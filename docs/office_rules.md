# Northwind Gadgets — Office Rules

## Office hours
The office is open 7:00–20:00 on weekdays. Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week**, agreed with their manager in advance.

## Guests
- Visitors must sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge at all times.
- Guests must be accompanied by an employee outside the lobby and meeting rooms.

## Kitchen and shared spaces
- Label food in the shared fridge with your name and date. The fridge is cleared every Friday at 17:00.
- Book meeting rooms through the calendar system; release them if your meeting is cancelled.
- Keep noise down in the quiet zone on the 3rd floor.

## Security
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk.
- Do not let anyone follow you through a badge-controlled door ("tailgating").
- The last person leaving the office must check that the main door is locked.
